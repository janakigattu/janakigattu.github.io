ArcGIS Enterprise Upgrade Planner

Most failed upgrades are not installer errors. They are version jumps Esri never supported, taken without a backup.

Enter your current version and your target version. This planner applies Esri’s published upgrade rules, works out whether you can go direct or need an interim version, puts the components in the right order, and hands you a checklist you can actually run. It is built from ten years of administering ArcGIS Enterprise, including upgrades at Caltrans.

Honest note: this planner runs entirely in your browser. No backend, no API key, nothing you enter leaves this page. It applies Esri’s published rules to the versions you enter. It does not inspect your environment, so check the system requirements for your target version before you book the window.
