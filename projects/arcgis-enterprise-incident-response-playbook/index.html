ArcGIS Enterprise Incident Response Playbook

An outage is not the time to work out what to do next. This is the page you open when the system is down: the severity, the steps in order, who to page, and the words for your users. Pick the incident, answer five questions, and print the playbook.

Runs entirely in your browser. No backend, no API key, nothing you type is sent or stored anywhere. Severity bands and response targets are planning rules from practice, not Esri numbers. The runbook steps come from Esri published guidance, cited below.

How the severity is decided

Three bands, and the rule behind each one. Severity is set by what users lose, not by which component failed.

  • SEV-1: core work is blocked for many users, or data loss is possible. Respond in 15 minutes, all hands, page the on-call admin now.
  • SEV-2: work is degraded or a smaller group is blocked. Respond in 1 hour. One admin owns it, a second on standby.
  • SEV-3: a small group, after hours, with a workaround. Respond within 4 business hours. One admin, no page.

A read-only data store and a restore are always SEV-1: edits cannot be saved, or the system is down until the restore finishes. Five hundred or more users affected is always SEV-1, whatever the fault.
