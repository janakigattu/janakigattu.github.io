Esri COTS App Planner

Most ArcGIS apps fail before anyone builds them: the wrong app for the job, or the right app on data that is not ready. Pick the job, the audience, and what you have, and this planner tells you which ArcGIS app to build, which widgets it needs, and how many days it takes. Everything here is a standard Esri app. No code, no custom widget, no server to stand up.

Runs entirely in your browser. No backend, no API key, no account. The widget names come from Esri documentation, and the day estimates and readiness scores are planning rules from practice, not vendor numbers. Nothing you type is sent or stored anywhere.

How the planner picks the app and prices the work

  1. The job picks the app. Collecting data in the field is Field Maps, or Survey123 when it is a form with no offline need. Telling a story is a StoryMap. Numbers for managers is a Dashboard. Comparing two maps is Experience Builder with the Swipe widget. Searching, filtering, or showing data on a map is Experience Builder.
  2. The widgets come from the job. Experience Builder widgets are the ones Esri documents: Map, Search, List, Filter, Query, Chart, Table, Swipe, Print, Survey, Basemap Gallery, Map Layers, plus Text and Button for the layout. Dashboard elements are Map, Indicator, Gauge, Serial chart, Pie chart, List, Details, Embedded Content, and Selectors. StoryMap blocks are Sidecar, Map tour, Swipe, and Timeline.
  3. Base effort, in person-days. Experience Builder 6, Dashboard 4, StoryMap 5, Field Maps 7, Survey123 3. Those are planning rules from practice, for one person who knows the tools.
  4. Data prep adds days. Spreadsheets to convert add 2. Nothing published yet adds 3. Live feeds add 2. No hosted layers and no web maps add 2.
  5. Interactions add half a day each past the third. The first three interactions are in the base price. Every one after that is another widget to configure and test.
  6. Readiness gaps add one day each. Data not cleaned, text not approved, no one named to maintain it: one day each to close.
  7. The readiness score. Start at 100. Lose 15 points per missing readiness item, 10 when nothing is published yet, 5 for spreadsheets to convert, and 5 when there are no hosted layers and no web maps. 75 or higher is Ready to build, 50 to 74 is Build after the data prep, below 50 is Decide the scope first.
  8. The phases. Scope and data prep 30 percent, build 40 percent, test with the audience 15 percent, publish 5 percent, handover and maintenance 10 percent.

Sources: Experience Builder widgets, Widgets, ArcGIS Experience Builder documentation. StoryMap blocks, Types of story blocks in ArcGIS StoryMaps. Dashboard elements, ArcGIS Dashboards documentation.
