AI Risk Register Builder

The risk nobody writes down is the one that bites. Pick the risks that apply to your assistant, rate how likely each one is and how bad it would be, and this builder gives you a scored risk register: every risk ranked, the top three to fix first, a mitigation and a named owner for each risk, and a register you can print.

Runs entirely in your browser. No backend, no API key, no account. The likelihood and impact scales, the bands, and the posture score are planning rules from practice, not vendor numbers, and the page says so wherever they are used. Nothing you type is sent or stored anywhere.

The ten risks

Tick the risks that apply to your assistant, then rate each one. Likelihood is how often you expect it to happen, impact is how bad it is when it does.

The assistant states something wrong as if it were fact, and nobody checks it before it reaches a user.

The answer comes from a draft, an old version, or the wrong document entirely, and it reads as confident as a right one.

Staff paste names, addresses, case details, or other restricted data into the assistant, and it leaves your control.

Instructions hidden in a document the assistant reads tell it to do something nobody asked for, like revealing restricted content.

Usage grows past the budget and nobody notices until the bill arrives, because no ceiling and no monthly check are in place.

The model or the documents change, the answers drift, and nobody retests, so the assistant quietly gets worse.

Nobody can explain why the assistant gave an answer, because the question, the answer, and the source were never logged.

People stop checking the answers, so the one wrong answer that matters goes out unchallenged.

The assistant repeats a bias in the training data or the documents, and the team only finds out when someone complains.

The assistant only works with one provider's model, so a price rise or a retirement leaves the team with nowhere to go.

How the builder scores risk

  1. Risk score. Likelihood times impact, from 1 to 25. Likelihood runs 1 Rare, 2 Unlikely, 3 Possible, 4 Likely, 5 Almost certain. Impact runs 1 Minor, 2 Moderate, 3 Serious, 4 Major, 5 Severe.
  2. Bands. 1 to 4 is Low, 5 to 9 is Medium, 10 to 15 is High, 16 to 25 is Critical. A risk only reaches Critical when it is both likely and serious, which is the point: the register should stay short enough to work from.
  3. Posture score. Start at 100 and subtract 12 per Critical risk, 7 per High, 3 per Medium, and 1 per Low. The score never drops below 0.
  4. Verdict. 85 or higher is Ready to proceed, 60 to 84 is Proceed with controls, below 60 is Stop and fix first.
  5. The Critical downgrade. One Critical risk drops the verdict a full band, whatever the score says. A register that scores 90 with a Critical risk still reads Proceed with controls, because a Critical risk is not a detail to carry into go-live.
  6. Owners. Every risk gets a named owner, not a team. The owner is the person who checks the mitigation is done and re-rates the risk after it is done.
