# AI Readiness Assessment

Most AI projects fail on the data, not the model. Ten questions show whether your team is ready before you spend anything.

## What it does

A working guided assessment, built by Janaki Gattu. Answer 10 questions across 6 areas, one at a time, and get:

- A score out of 100
- A red, amber, or green status for each of the six areas
- Your top 3 gaps to close, each with a fix
- Recommended next steps
- Your full answer list

The six areas: use cases and value, data readiness, people and skills, governance and trust, technology and integration, and change readiness.

## How to run it

Open `index.html` in a browser. That is all. It runs entirely in your browser: no server, no backend, no API key, no account, and nothing you answer leaves the page. Your score is calculated on your device and is not stored or sent anywhere.

## What it is built from

Janaki Gattu's article [Harness Engineering: the discipline that separates production AI agents from demos](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/harness-engineering-discipline-separates-production-ai-janaki-gattu-jdcdc/), and her AI readiness assessment service: the same questions she asks a team before scoping a pilot, asked here before the build instead of after it.
