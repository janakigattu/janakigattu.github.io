AI Readiness Assessment

Most AI projects fail on the data, not the model. Ten questions show whether your team is ready before you spend anything.

How this assessment works, honestly: it runs entirely in your browser. There is no server, no API key, no account, and nothing you answer leaves this page. Your score is calculated here, on this device, and it is not stored or sent anywhere.

1. Use cases and value

One job named first, with a number that says whether it worked, and a business owner who wants the result.

2. Data readiness

The data the assistant needs, in one place, current, clean, and with a written list of what it must never touch.

3. People and skills

Someone who can spot a wrong answer, and time in their week to review, correct, and improve the assistant after launch.

4. Governance and trust

What happens when the assistant is wrong, and where your data goes when it runs.

5. Technology and integration

Whether the assistant can reach the systems it needs, and whether it works where your team already works.

6. Change readiness

Whether the team will stay with a pilot that is wrong half the time at first, and improve it, instead of walking away.

What you get in about five minutes

Ten questions, one at a time. At the end you get a score out of 100, a red, amber, or green status for each of the six areas, your top three gaps to close, and recommended next steps.

  • Readiness is not about the model. It is about the job, the data, the reviewer, and the plan for when the answer is wrong. A team that scores well here can start small and learn fast.
  • The result is a short list of fixes, ranked by risk, with an owner and a date for each one. Not fifty items. Five or six, the ones that matter most.
  • If you are not sure where to start, start with the use case. Name the one job, name the person who owns the outcome, and write down the number that says it worked.
