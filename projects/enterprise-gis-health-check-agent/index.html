Enterprise GIS Health Check

Most teams only check their ArcGIS Enterprise deployment after something breaks, usually on a Monday morning. Take ten minutes now and find the cracks before they break.

How this assessment works, honestly: it runs entirely in your browser. There is no server, no API key, no account, and nothing you answer leaves this page. Your score is calculated here, on this device, and it is not stored or sent anywhere.

1. Uptime and monitoring

Portal, Server, and the Data Store running and responding, with logs someone actually reads.

2. Backups and disaster recovery

Backups you have restored, not just backups you have taken.

3. Security and access control

Who has access, and to what. No accounts for people who left months ago.

4. Data quality

Broken layers and content nobody owns, found before a user finds them mid-task.

5. Performance

Which services are slow, and when. What people use, and what nobody has opened in a year.

6. Upgrades and patches

Patch lag closed before it becomes a support and security problem.

7. Documentation and governance

Runbooks, owners, and a short ranked list of fixes with a date for each one.

What you get in about five minutes

Ten questions, one at a time. At the end you get a score out of 100, a red, amber, or green status for each of the seven areas, your top three gaps to close, and recommended next steps.

  • A light check every quarter, and a deeper one once a year. That rhythm is the point.
  • The result is a short list of fixes, ranked by risk, with an owner and a date for each one. Not fifty items. Five or six, the ones that matter most.
  • If you are not sure where to start, start with one area this week. Pick the one that worries you most.
