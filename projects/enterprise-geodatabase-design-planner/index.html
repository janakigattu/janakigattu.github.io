Enterprise Geodatabase Design Planner

The versioning choice you make on day one decides what your editors can and cannot do for the next ten years.

An enterprise geodatabase holds the data every map, app, and service reads. This planner takes how your team edits and turns it into the design: branch or traditional versioning, archiving, editor tracking, replicas, and the upkeep the design needs. Nothing leaves your browser.

Honest note: this page runs entirely in your browser. There is no backend, no API key, and nothing is sent anywhere. The versioning, archiving, and replication rules come from Esri's published ArcGIS Pro documentation, linked below. The effort figures are planning rules from practice, not Esri numbers, and the page labels them as such.
