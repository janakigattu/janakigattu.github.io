AI Use Case Prioritizer

Every team has ten ideas for AI and budget for one pilot. Pick the wrong first pilot and you burn the budget and the trust. List the candidates, score each one on value, data, effort, and risk, and start with the one that can actually ship.

How this prioritizer works, honestly: it runs entirely in your browser. There is no server, no API key, no account, and nothing you enter leaves this page. The scoring is a planning rule from practice, not a vendor benchmark: value is the weekly hours at stake, feasibility is data readiness plus the effort and the risk of getting it wrong. Change any number and the ranking follows.

What to do with each quadrant

    How the score is built

    Weekly hours at stake is hours saved per person per week times people affected. Value, out of 50, is that number against the best candidate in your list. Feasibility, out of 50, is data readiness (0 to 20), effort inverted (0 to 15), and risk inverted (0 to 15). The priority score is value plus feasibility, out of 100. A quadrant needs 25 or more on both halves to be a quick win. The weights are a planning rule from practice, stated here so you can disagree with them.
