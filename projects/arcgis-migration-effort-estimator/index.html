ArcGIS Migration Effort Estimator

A migration is not one job. It is a pile of small jobs wearing one name, and the pile is different every time. Start with scope, not the tool. Count what you actually have to move between ArcGIS Enterprise and ArcGIS Online, and this estimator prices the move in person-days, names the biggest workstream, and flags the things that set the schedule.

How this estimator works, honestly: it runs entirely in your browser. There is no server, no API key, no account, and nothing you enter leaves this page. The day rates are planning rules from practice, not Esri numbers: Esri publishes no migration effort rates, so treat this as a scoping estimate, not a quote. The facts it rests on are Janaki’s migration article and Esri’s own documentation, both cited below.

Which way are you moving?

The direction changes the work: Enterprise to Online means republishing what referenced registered data and rebuilding what Online does not carry. Online to Enterprise means standing up the deployment and the licences first.

ArcGIS Enterprise → ArcGIS Online Retire the servers, keep the content. Hosted data moves; referenced data, extensions, and the Utility Network do not come as they are.
ArcGIS Online → ArcGIS Enterprise Bring the content home. Users and licences first, content through collaboration, and the Online-only pieces rebuilt.
