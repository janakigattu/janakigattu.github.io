AI Cost Estimator

The pilot was cheap. The running bill is the surprise. Price the month before you commit to it. Enter how much your assistant will actually be used, and this estimator prices the model, the human review, and the hosting, names the biggest cost driver, and tells you what to change first.

How this estimator works, honestly: it runs entirely in your browser. There is no server, no API key, no account, and nothing you enter leaves this page. The three model tiers start from the providers’ own published list prices, checked 26 November 2026. Providers reprice and retire models without much notice, so confirm your model’s current rates on its pricing page before you build a budget on this. Treat the result as a planning estimate, not a bill.

Human review

Someone has to check the answers. In most pilots this costs more than the model, and it is the line most budgets forget.

How many answers a person reads and checks each day. Zero means nobody reviews, which is a decision, not a saving.
