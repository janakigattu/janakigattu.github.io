What you are looking at

Before you sign a lease or buy a home in Sacramento, check this map first. A home five minutes from a light rail station costs you less every single day than the same home a mile from the nearest bus stop.

The map puts housing and transit on the same screen, because that is how the decision actually works. Apartment locations from Sacramento County open data sit alongside every transit stop in the Caltrans GTFS feed and the SacRT light rail lines and stations from SACOG. A home is not just a price and a floor plan. It is a commute, a bus fare, and a walk in the rain.

How to read transit access

Apartment complex (Sacramento County open data)
Transit stop, bus and light rail (Caltrans GTFS)
Gold Line light rail
Blue Line light rail
Green Line light rail
Light rail station (SACOG)

The rule of thumb planners use: about 400 metres is a five-minute walk, and about 800 metres is a ten-minute walk. Click anywhere on the map and the app tells you what is within those distances of that spot: the nearest apartment complex, the number of transit stops within a five-minute walk, and the nearest light rail station. Light rail is the high-frequency spine of the system, so a home near a station is worth more in daily time saved than a home near a single bus stop.

What the layers show

Use the layer switcher at the top right of the map to turn layers on and off, and switch between the topographic and imagery basemaps. Transit stops come from the statewide Caltrans GTFS feed, so they only draw for the area you are looking at. Light rail lines and stations come from SACOG's Regional Transit Network Plan for the Sacramento region.

What this map does not do

  • It is not a listings site. It shows where apartment complexes are, not which units are available or what they cost.
  • A stop on the map is not a promise of frequent service. Check the route timetable before you rely on a stop, and check how many arrivals a day it actually gets.
  • Transit data is schedule data, not real time. A delayed bus does not show up here.
  • Walking distance is measured as the crow flies, not along streets. A stop 400 metres away across a freeway is not a five-minute walk.

Data sources

  • Apartments, Sacramento County open data, ArcGIS REST service
  • California Transit Stops, Caltrans GTFS feed via California Open Data (data last updated November 19, 2025)
  • Light Rail lines and stations, SACOG Regional Transit Network Plan, ArcGIS REST service
  • Basemaps: Esri World Topo and World Imagery
