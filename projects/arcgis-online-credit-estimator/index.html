ArcGIS Online Credit Estimator

Most ArcGIS Online bills are a surprise because nobody added up the credits first. Add up the month before you spend it. Enter your storage and your transactions, and this estimator prices them at Esri’s published credit rates, names the biggest cost driver, and tells you how to bring the bill down.

How this estimator works, honestly: it runs entirely in your browser. There is no server, no API key, no account, and nothing you enter leaves this page. Every rate is Esri’s published rate from the Understand credits documentation, checked 25 November 2026. Esri can change the rates, and your real usage is on the organisation status dashboard, so treat this as a planning estimate, not a bill.

Start from a preset, or enter your own numbers

The presets are typical months, not promises. Change any number and the estimate follows.

Storage, per month

Storage is charged for the whole month, calculated hourly. Feature storage is the expensive one, and the one most teams underestimate.

Size of your hosted feature layers, in MB. Check the Storage tab on the status dashboard.

2.4 credits per 10 MB stored per month

Attachments, PDFs, images, tile packages, scene layer packages, and other files, in GB.

1.2 credits per 1 GB stored per month

Tiled imagery layers and map tile data, in GB.

1.2 credits per 1 GB stored per month

Files stored in ArcGIS Notebooks workspaces, in GB, and the number of users with workspaces.

12 credits per 1 GB stored per month, per user

Transactions, per month

Charged per job, per record, or per route. The small numbers are the trap: a half credit times ten thousand routes is a real bill.

Addresses matched with the ArcGIS World Geocoding Service, for example when publishing a spreadsheet as a hosted feature layer.

40 credits per 1,000 geocodes

Directions in Map Viewer, one origin to one destination.

0.005 credits per simple route

Directions with the stop order optimised.

0.5 credits per optimised route

Service areas, also called travel areas. One request for eight places at three distances is 24 areas, not one.

0.5 credits per drive-time area

Find Nearest routes, one per facility found.

0.5 credits per closest facility route

Plan Routes jobs, one per vehicle route.

1 credit per vehicle route

Data variables multiplied by feature records. Enriching 1,000 records with 4 variables is 4,000 attributes.

10 credits per 1,000 attributes

Features analysed by feature analysis tools in Map Viewer, for example Aggregate Points or Join Features.

1 credit per 1,000 features

Tiles built for a tile cache with ArcGIS Online.

1 credit per 10,000 tiles generated

Pan, zoom, and identify requests on maps with Esri demographic data.

10 credits per 1,000 map requests

Infographics viewed in Business Analyst, Experience Builder, ArcGIS Pro, and other supported apps.

10 credits per 1,000 views

Maps printed from custom layout templates in Map Viewer. Standard prints do not consume credits.

5 credits per print job

Hours connected to a ModelBuilder analysis session. Charged per hour with a 10 minute minimum.

50 credits per hour

Images analysed with AI, for example with analyze_image in the ArcGIS API for Python.

1 credit per 5 images

Characters translated with AI, for example with translate in the ArcGIS API for Python or TranslateText in Arcade.

1 credit per 1,000 characters

Your subscription

Credits included with your annual subscription depend on the user types you buy. A Creator user type includes 500 credits, a Contributor 250. Additional credits can be bought in blocks of 1,000.

Total credits your annual subscription includes. Find it on your organisation status dashboard.

Unused subscription credits expire at the end of the subscription period.

Using Living Atlas basemaps and imagery, creating and sharing maps and apps, managing data, exporting data, and single-address or place searches. Most of what a team does every day is free.

Sources

If a rate here disagrees with the documentation, the documentation wins.

Two numbers worth remembering from that same page: it costs fewer than 5 credits to geocode 125 addresses, and fewer than 5 credits to store 2 GB of map tile data. The bill only becomes a problem when the small numbers repeat every day.
