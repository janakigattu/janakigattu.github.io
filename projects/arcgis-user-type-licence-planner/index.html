ArcGIS User Type and Licence Planner

Every ArcGIS Online bill tells the same story: too many people on the wrong licence. Count your team by what they actually do, and this planner names the cheapest user type that covers each job, with the annual bill to prove it.

Runs entirely in your browser. No backend, no API key, no account. Prices are Esri US list prices per user per year, read 23 Nov 2026, and you can edit every rate on this page. Prices move, so confirm the current rates with your Esri representative before you budget. The role mapping follows Esri's own user type rules. Nothing you type is sent or stored anywhere.

Step 1: Count your team by what they do

Count people by their job, not by the licence they hold today. The planner maps each job to the cheapest user type that covers it.

Step 2: The two jobs that move people up a tier

Two small facts change the answer more than any other. A viewer who edits needs Contributor, not Viewer. A creator who needs Pro Standard needs Professional, not Creator.

Step 3: Check the rates

Esri US list prices per user per year, read 23 Nov 2026. Edit any rate to match your quote, then press Plan my licences.

How the plan is made

  1. Each job maps to one user type. Viewers view only. Field crew get Mobile Worker. Editors get Contributor. Map builders get Creator. Analysts get Professional. Advanced analysts get Professional Plus.
  2. The cheapest type that covers the job wins. A Viewer cannot create, edit, share, or analyse. A Contributor cannot analyse, create, or share. A Mobile Worker cannot analyse, create, or share. Creator is the first type that can do all of it, and it includes ArcGIS Pro Basic.
  3. The two moves in Step 2 are the ones teams get wrong. A viewer who edits on a Viewer licence cannot save the edit. A creator who needs Pro Standard on a Creator licence cannot open the tools. Both get fixed here, before the bill is counted.
  4. An organisation needs at least one Creator, Professional, or Professional Plus licence. That is the licence that creates the account and administers it. If your plan has none, the planner says so and adds one Creator to the bill.
  5. ArcGIS Enterprise uses the same user type names. Viewer, Editor, Mobile Worker, Creator, and GIS Professional (Basic, Standard, Advanced) work the same way in Portal. Enterprise licences are priced through your Enterprise Agreement, not these list prices, so take the counts in this plan to your Esri representative.
