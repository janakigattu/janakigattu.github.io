ArcGIS Enterprise Troubleshooting Agent

The map stopped loading at 4:55 pm on a Friday. This agent finds the cause before the phone starts ringing. Pick the symptom, answer three questions, and get the most likely cause, the checks that confirm it, and the fix.

How this agent works, honestly: it runs entirely in your browser. There is no server, no API key, no account, and nothing you answer leaves this page. It is a decision tree built from Esri’s own administration guidance and ten years of running these systems. It does not connect to your deployment, and it does not replace testing a fix in your own environment.

Three questions solve most failures

Before you open a case, or before you restart anything, answer these three. They narrow almost every Enterprise failure to one cause.

  • What changed? A certificate, a patch, an upgrade, a firewall rule, a password. Most failures start right after a change.
  • What do the logs say? The Portal logs and the Server logs name the failing component. Guessing without them wastes the afternoon.
  • How much disk is free? A full drive puts the relational Data Store in read-only mode, and that one condition breaks publishing, hosted layers, and backups at the same time.
