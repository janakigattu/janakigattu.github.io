How the planner scores adoption

  1. Pilot evidence, 25 points. A pilot that measured hours saved is worth the full 25. A pilot people liked but never measured is worth 15. No pilot is 0, because there is nothing to show the next team yet.
  2. Champions, 20 points. One named champion per team is 20. Some teams covered is 12. None is 0, because a rollout with no local champion is a broadcast, not an adoption.
  3. Training, 20 points. Two hours of hands-on training per user is 20. One hour is 12. None is 0.
  4. Comms, 15 points. Announcement 4, managers briefed 4, first success story 4, help channel live 3.
  5. Feedback, 20 points. A named owner is 10, a feedback channel someone reads is 10.
  6. Resistance, minus 3 per sign. Five signs, fifteen points at most, and the score never drops below 0.
  7. The forecast. Weekly active users grow 12 percent a week when the score is 75 or higher, 7 percent at 50 to 74, and 2 percent below 50. Those are planning rates from practice, not a promise.
  8. The training schedule. Fifteen users per session, two trainer hours per session. That is a planning rule, so check it against your own room size.
