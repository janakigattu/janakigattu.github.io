# AI Change Management Planner

The assistant is live, and nobody is using it. That is not a model problem, it is an adoption problem.

## What it does

Enter the rollout size, what the pilot proved, the champions, the training, the comms already done, the feedback loop, and the resistance you have actually heard, and get an adoption score out of 100, a verdict (Ready to scale, Scale in waves, or Fix the adoption gaps first), a twelve week forecast of weekly active users, a training schedule in sessions and hours, a six week comms plan that adapts to your answers, the three gaps to close first, the two fixes that move the answer fastest, and a printable checklist.

## How the score works

Pilot evidence 25, champions 20, training 20, comms 15, feedback 20, minus 3 points per resistance sign, floored at 0. Weekly growth is 12 percent at 75 or higher, 7 percent at 50 to 74, and 2 percent below 50. Training runs fifteen users per session at two trainer hours per session. All of those are planning rules from practice, not vendor numbers, and the page says so.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No backend, no API key, no account, nothing sent or stored. Three presets show the range: a small team with champions in place (100, Ready to scale), a department rollout with no champions yet (50, Scale in waves), and a regulated team with high resistance (1, Fix the adoption gaps first). Print button included.
