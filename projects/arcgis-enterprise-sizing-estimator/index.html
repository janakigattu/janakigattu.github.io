    What Esri publishes, and what this adds

    Esri publishes minimums only. Portal 11.5: 2 cores minimum, 4 highly recommended for production, 8 GB RAM, 20 GB disk. ArcGIS Server 11.5: 8 GB RAM per machine minimum, 10 GB disk, and no published production numbers. Data Store 11.5: 13 GB disk to install one empty type, and minimum free RAM of 8 GB for a relational store, 8 GB for a tile cache store, 16 GB for a spatiotemporal store, and 32 GB for a graph store. Below 10 GB free disk the data store logs warnings. Below 5 GB free the relational, spatiotemporal, and object stores go read-only and the graph store shuts down. Everything above those minimums on this page is a planning rule from practice: Portal scales with named users, the hosting server scales with concurrent users and editors, the relational store scales with data volume, and disk carries a full backup copy plus growth headroom. Treat the totals as the spec to take to procurement, then confirm with a load test.
