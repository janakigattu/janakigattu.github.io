Four Topics

Four topics, each on its own card.

The four subjects teams ask Janaki about most, each on its own card.

Topic 01 · AI Assistants in GIS

01

Twelve assistants already live inside ArcGIS. Three are ready for everyone.

The skill to build now is knowing which routine tasks to hand to an assistant, and how to validate what comes back.

  • Three are generally available at no extra cost: Survey123, Business Analyst, and Translation.
  • The rest are in beta or preview, including the Pro, Notebooks, and Arcade assistants.
  • Most value today arrives through ArcGIS Online. The Item details assistant is the only one in Enterprise, and only in 12.0.
  • Access is governed at the organisation level. An administrator must opt in to AI features.
Esri: What’s new in AI assistants →Download the guide (PDF) →

Topic 02 · Migration in ArcGIS Enterprise

02

Start with scope, not the tool.

Migration is rarely one kind of job. Name the move first, and the tool choice gets easier.

  • Three kinds of move: a full environment, selected content, or a change of product or platform.
  • Six approaches, compared honestly: WebGISDR, Join Site, group export and import, distributed collaboration, scripting, and rebuild.
  • Eight questions to answer in writing before you choose an approach.
  • Build the target, test it with real users, and keep a rollback route you have actually practised.
Read the LinkedIn article →Listen to Episode 1 →

Topic 03 · Migration in ArcGIS Online

03

Online and Enterprise are not two copies of the same system.

The move between them needs its own scope: users, licences, content, and what gets rebuilt.

  • Distributed collaboration shares Enterprise content to Online, as a reference or as a copy.
  • Partnered collaboration is the mechanism built for Online-to-Online sharing.
  • Scripted moves use clone_items() or OfflineContentManager in the ArcGIS API for Python.
  • Group export and import is Enterprise only. Maps and apps still point at the source layer URLs until you update them.
Esri: Collaborate between Enterprise, Online and Field Maps →Download the guide (PDF) →

Topic 04 · ArcGIS Monitor

04

Find the problem before your users do.

Monitor turns system health, usage, and performance into numbers you can act on, before the phone rings.

  • Three components: Monitor Server, Monitor Agent, and the database repository.
  • Metrics with alert rules on every component, from request rate to CPU and disk.
  • Eight statuses, from nominal to critical, set by the most severe metric.
  • As of Enterprise 12.2, Monitor also covers ArcGIS Velocity.
Esri documentation: Introduction to ArcGIS Monitor →Download the guide (PDF) →
